# Sources: Sandpapergate (Sport, by the numbers) — 20 Nov 2026

House rule override: on 15 Nov 2026 "Sport, by the numbers" adopted a rule of "no accusations about
referees or players." Shad explicitly overrode that rule for this one video (20 Nov 2026) after being
shown the conflict, on condition every claim is attributed to its source rather than stated as fact.

- 2018 bans (Steve Smith 12 months, David Warner 12 months + barred from future leadership, Cameron
  Bancroft 9 months + 75% match fee): Cricket Australia's own 2018 investigation findings. Undisputed.
- Usman Khawaja's memoir "Sabr" (published 9 Nov 2026): claims wider knowledge in the squad. Per Pat
  Cummins's own reading of the book at his press conference, it calls Cummins and others "architects of
  a scheme." (Daily Maverick, 13 Nov 2026 / EWN, 13 Nov 2026)
- Pat Cummins, press conference, Sydney, 13 Nov 2026: "If by that he means that we knew sandpaper was
  going out in the field, he's 100% wrong." Called the "scheme" claim "complete and utter nonsense." Said
  he was "genuinely shocked" by Khawaja's separate claim about Bancroft. (Daily Maverick, 13 Nov 2026)
- David Warner, Instagram + Kayo Cricket, 14 Nov 2026: called Khawaja's account "100 per cent correct";
  claimed Cricket Australia never interviewed Hazlewood, Starc or Lyon in 2018; said the investigation
  "stinks of a cover-up." (The Nightly, 14 Nov 2026)
- Cricket Australia (CEO Todd Greenberg, paraphrased, 10 Nov 2026): ruled out reopening the 2018
  investigation; said some accounts from that squad "differ substantially" from Khawaja's.
- Bowlers' joint statement (Cummins, Starc, Hazlewood, Lyon), 18 May 2021: "We did not know a foreign
  substance was taken onto the field" before it appeared on the big screen at Newlands.
- Statement reported 18 Nov 2026 and attributed to Cameron Bancroft, ~4,000 words: reported to say the
  entire team knew. Not independently confirmed by Cricket Australia as of this video. Treated here as a
  reported claim, not a verified fact — per house practice, "show source conflicts, don't silently pick a
  side" (see src/videos/penaltyTry.ts).
- "4 players now disputed CA's account": Khawaja, Warner, Bancroft (per the above) and Chadd Sayers
  (former teammate, publicly backed Khawaja's account, 15 Nov 2026).

No view taken in the video on who is telling the truth. Every number and quote above traces to a dated
source; nothing is asserted as settled except the undisputed 2018 CA findings.
