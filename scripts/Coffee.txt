One coffee. Five dollars. Every day, for thirty years. What does it really cost?

Meet Jordan and Riley. Jordan buys the coffee. Riley skips it, and invests the same money instead. About a hundred and fifty dollars a month.

For the first few years, there's barely any difference. But watch what happens around year nineteen. The growth on Riley's money overtakes everything Riley has put in.

After thirty years, Jordan has spent fifty-four thousand, seven hundred and fifty dollars on coffee. Riley has a hundred and eighty-five thousand dollars. Same five dollars a day. More than a hundred and thirty thousand dollars apart.

And most of Riley's money? It's growth. Riley never earned more than Jordan. Time did the heavy lifting.

So enjoy the coffee. Just know its real price. That's the sum of it.
