In Lisbon, Manchester United thought they'd won it late. Then VAR stepped in. Here's how the sum added up.

Two all, late on. A United corner. Sporting keeper Kaique goes up, and the ball ends in his own net.

Then VAR sends referee Szymon Marciniak to the monitor. He rules Patrick Dorgu fouled the keeper. No goal.

UEFA's explanation, in one report: Dorgu fouled the keeper before the ball went in. Pundits saw a keeper who flapped at it before he was touched.

Under the VAR protocol, video can step in only for a clear and obvious error. And the final decision is always the referee's.

It was United's second goal ruled out. Before half-time, Amad Diallo had the ball in the net, but the whistle had already gone for a Tielemans foul. So VAR couldn't help.

Three penalties, two goals chalked off, and a late red for Tielemans. Two all. United nineteenth.
