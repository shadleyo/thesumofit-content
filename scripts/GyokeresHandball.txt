At the Emirates, Dortmund wanted a handball penalty. The referee played on. Here's how the sum added up.

Viktor Gyokeres puts Arsenal one nil up early. Second half, a Dortmund corner. Inside the Arsenal box, the ball hits Gyokeres on the arm.

Referee Maurizio Mariani plays on. No penalty, and no trip to the monitor. Reports differ on whether VAR even checked it.

Under Law twelve, not every touch on the arm is handball. It's an offence if the arm made the body unnaturally bigger, and its position wasn't justified by the player's movement.

One former referee, Keith Hackett, says Gyokeres moved his arm towards the ball. Another, Patrick Ittrich, calls it a clear penalty. Niko Kovac says VAR should have looked.

Four minutes later, Maximilian Beier equalises. One one. A point each. Dortmund seventh on ten. Arsenal fifth on eleven.
