Thirteen states could decide who controls the Senate on November third. In every one of them, gas costs far more than it did before the Iran war.

The biggest jumps are in the heartland. Montana, up a dollar sixty-seven a gallon. Nebraska, up a dollar sixty-four. Iowa, a dollar fifty. That's around sixty percent more than before the war.

Fill a fifteen gallon tank in Michigan, and it costs about twenty-two dollars more than in February. In Maine, twenty-one dollars more. Even in North Carolina, where the rise is smallest, it's twelve dollars more, every single fill-up.

Here are all thirteen. In eleven of them, gas is up more than a dollar a gallon. Nationally, the average household has paid about eight hundred and seventy dollars extra for gas and diesel since the war began.
