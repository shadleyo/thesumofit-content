A thirty thousand dollar car. At nine percent. How long you borrow for changes the price.

Over three years, you pay four thousand, three hundred dollars in interest. Over five years, seven thousand, four hundred. Over seven years, ten thousand, five hundred.

The monthly payment drops. The total cost climbs. Stretch it to seven years, and you pay thirty-five percent of the car again, just in interest.

A lower payment isn't a lower price. That's the sum of it.
