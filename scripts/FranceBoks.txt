The Springboks lost by two points. Here are the numbers.

France twenty-one, South Africa nineteen, in Paris.

At half-time it was twenty-one to seven. In the second half, France scored nothing, and South Africa scored twelve.

Five cards in the match. One red, four yellow. France played twenty-eight minutes of the second half a player short.

And the Boks won four scrum penalties in a row, between the sixty-first and sixty-fifth minutes.

A try is worth five points. The margin was two.
