The fifty, thirty, twenty rule. Split your take-home pay three ways.

Fifty percent for needs: rent, food, transport. Thirty percent for wants. Twenty percent for saving and paying off debt. On four thousand dollars a month, that's two thousand for needs, twelve hundred for wants, and eight hundred saved.

Invest that eight hundred for thirty years at seven percent, and it grows to about nine hundred and seventy-six thousand dollars.

It's a starting point, not a rule to obey. That's the sum of it.
