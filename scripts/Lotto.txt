The lottery is a tax on people who can't do maths. Here's why.

The odds of winning the Lotto jackpot: one in twenty million, three hundred and fifty-eight thousand. You are fifty-eight times more likely to be struck by lightning.

Say you spend twenty rand a week on tickets. Over forty years, that's forty-one thousand, six hundred rand.

Invest the same twenty rand a week at seven percent instead, and you end with about two hundred and twenty-seven thousand rand. Guaranteed by maths, not luck.

Play for fun if you like. Just don't call it a plan. That's the sum of it.
