In Lyon, the penalty was given. Then VAR took it away. Here's how the sum added up.

Nil-nil against Lillestrom, in the closing minutes. Substitute Loïs Openda runs into the box, between two defenders, and goes down.

Referee Sebastian Gishamer points to the spot. Then VAR calls him to the screen. He changes his mind. No penalty.

The reason, in one report: the first defender had already played the ball before the second one fouled. Lyon's coach says that touch was very light, and Openda could still reach it. Lyon's Félix Bacher: if I were the referee, I'd have given it.

Under the VAR protocol, video can step in only for a clear and obvious error. And the final decision is always the referee's.

It wasn't the night's only reversal. An early Lyon goal, ruled out for a foul on the keeper. A late Lillestrom red card, cancelled because the defender got the ball first.

Nil-nil. Eight points. Lyon fourteenth or fifteenth, depending on the report.
