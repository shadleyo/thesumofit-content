How much do you need to retire?

A common rule of thumb is the four percent rule. Take four percent of your savings each year, and historically, the money has tended to last about thirty years. Flip it around, and you need twenty-five times what you spend in a year.

Two thousand dollars a month needs six hundred thousand. Three thousand needs nine hundred thousand. Five thousand needs one point five million.

It's a starting point, not a guarantee. But it turns a vague goal into a number. That's the sum of it.
