Zero percent for twelve months. Sounds free. And it can be, if you clear it in time.

Spend three thousand dollars, and pay one hundred and fifty a month. When the twelve months end, one thousand, two hundred dollars is still owed.

Now the rate jumps to twenty-two percent. Keep paying one hundred and fifty, and that last twelve hundred takes nine more months, and costs one hundred and ten dollars in interest.

The fix: divide the balance by the months you have. Three thousand over twelve months is two hundred and fifty a month. Done before the clock runs out. That's the sum of it.
