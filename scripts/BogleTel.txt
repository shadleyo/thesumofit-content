Three votes to two. That's how close a Premier League panel came to calling this a red card.

Leeds against Tottenham, thirty-fourth minute. Jayden Bogle goes in on Mathys Tel.

Where did it land? The panel says the back of the ankle. The Press Association's report says high on the Achilles, with his studs.

Referee John Brooks gives a free kick. No card. VAR checks it, but doesn't send him to the screen.

Tel can't carry on. He's off before half-time. Leeds win one-nil.

Then the five-person panel votes. Three say: reckless, but without the speed, force or intensity for a red. Two say it was dangerous enough.

Under Law Twelve, reckless means a yellow. Endangering an opponent means a red. Bogle got neither.

Three to two. Right call, or one vote from a red? You be the panel. Follow for the next sum.
