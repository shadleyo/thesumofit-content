On the campaign trail in 2024, Donald Trump made five promises about prices. Here's each one, next to the latest official number.

One. Petrol. The promise: "gasoline below two dollars a gallon". The latest national average: four dollars thirty-seven. The Iran war pushed oil up this year.

Two. Groceries. The promise: "your groceries will be cheaper". Food at home: up four percent since November 2024.

Three. Mortgages. The promise: rates back to two percent. The thirty-year average: seven point two eight percent. Higher than when he took office.

Four. Electricity. The promise: cut energy and electricity prices "by half". Residential electricity costs: up twelve and a half percent.

Five. Inflation. The promise: to defeat it. The latest annual rate: three point seven percent. The Fed's target is two.

Some numbers did move the promised way. Credit card rates are slightly lower. Prescription drug prices are down almost four percent from their peak.

Five promises. The numbers are public. You decide. That's the sum of it.
