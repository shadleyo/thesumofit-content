Inside ten minutes, Manchester United were down to ten. Here's how the sum added up at Anfield.

A long ball over the top. Matthijs de Ligt misjudges it. Alexander Isak is through.

Leny Yoro gets back, and brings him down, just outside the box. Referee Sam Barrott shows a straight red. Denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity. VAR checks it, and confirms.

Under Law Twelve, that call weighs four things. Distance to goal. Direction of play. The chance of keeping or gaining control of the ball. And where the defenders are.

The third one is the argument. Reports say Isak's touch was heavy, towards the keeper. The officials say it was still a chance. United's manager and players say it was never a red.

Dominik Szoboszlai scores the free kick. Florian Wirtz adds a second. Two more after the break. Four, nil.
