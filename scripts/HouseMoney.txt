CNN projects that Democrats will win the US House. Here's what that does, and doesn't, change for your money.

The new House is sworn in on January third. Until then, the current Congress still decides, and federal funding runs out on December eleventh. That's the first fight.

From January, any new war money needs the House. The Pentagon has asked for sixty-seven billion dollars more for the Iran war. That request would now need Democratic votes.

Tax bills must start in the House too, under the Constitution. And with the national debt near forty trillion dollars, the debt limit comes due again next year.

What the House can't easily change: tariffs, which are now set mostly by the president, and gas prices, which follow oil and the war. Overriding a veto takes two thirds of both chambers.

And the Senate is still too close to call.

The election is over. The budget fights are just beginning. That's the sum of it.
