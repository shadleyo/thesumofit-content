Two friends. Same two hundred dollars a month. One starts at twenty-five, saves for ten years, then stops completely. The other starts at thirty-five and saves for thirty years straight. Who ends up with more?

Meet Maya and Leo. Maya puts in twenty-four thousand dollars in total. Leo puts in seventy-two thousand. Three times as much.

Watch the race. Maya stops at thirty-five, but her money keeps growing. Leo starts, and chases, and never quite catches up.

At sixty-five, Maya has two hundred and eighty-one thousand dollars. Leo has two hundred and forty-four thousand.

A third of the effort, and more money. Because Maya's money had ten extra years to compound. Time did the heavy lifting.

The best time to start was yesterday. The next best is today. That's the sum of it.
