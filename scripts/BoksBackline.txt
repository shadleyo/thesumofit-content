South Africa are in the final. But in Dublin, the reports agree: the backline barely fired. Here it is, by the numbers.

Two Springbok tries. One from a five-metre scrum. One by the hooker, Malcolm Marx. Ireland's wing Robert Baloucoune scored two on his own.

His first: a pass from McCloskey, a step inside the Springbok fullback, and over.

In the air, three reports say the same thing. Ireland won the high ball. The Bok wings lost those duels.

One stats sheet has Ireland passing a hundred and forty-nine times. South Africa, a hundred and seven.

Assistant coach Felix Jones said Ireland capitalised on a couple of their exit kicks.
