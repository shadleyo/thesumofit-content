A million dollars today, or a single penny that doubles every day for thirty days. Which would you take?

Meet Jordan and Riley. Jordan takes the million, right now. Riley takes the penny, and lets it double every day for a month.

Watch the race. For weeks, Riley's penny looks like a terrible deal. On day twenty, it's worth just five thousand two hundred dollars. Then it explodes, and passes the million on day twenty-eight.

On day thirty, Jordan still has one million dollars. Riley has five point three seven million.

Half of Riley's money arrived on the very last day. That's the shape of compounding. Slow, slow, then sudden.

Real investments don't double every day. But the curve looks the same. Give it time. That's the sum of it.
