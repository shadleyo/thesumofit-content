Five subscriptions. Fifteen dollars each. Seventy-five dollars a month. What do they really cost?

Meet Sam and Kai. Sam keeps all five. Kai cancels them, and invests the seventy-five dollars instead.

For years, the gap looks small. But around year nineteen, Kai's growth overtakes everything Kai put in.

After twenty years, Sam has spent eighteen thousand dollars. Kai has thirty-nine thousand. Same seventy-five dollars a month. Twenty-one thousand dollars apart.

Keep the ones you use. Cancel the ones you forgot. That's the sum of it.
