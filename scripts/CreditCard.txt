You owe five thousand dollars on a credit card, at twenty-two percent interest. If you only pay the minimum, how long until it's gone?

Meet Sam and Alex. Same card. Same debt. Sam pays the minimum, about a hundred and forty dollars to start, shrinking as the balance falls. Alex pays a fixed two hundred dollars, every month.

Watch the balances. Alex is debt-free in under three years. Sam? Still paying. And paying. For more than nineteen years.

Sam pays eight thousand one hundred dollars in interest. Alex pays one thousand seven hundred and fifty. Paying less each month cost Sam six thousand three hundred and fifty dollars more.

That's the trick of the minimum payment. It shrinks as your balance shrinks, so your debt shrinks slowly too.

Pay more than the minimum. Even a little helps. That's the sum of it.
