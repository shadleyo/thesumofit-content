Las Vegas, Friday practice. Verstappen and Russell collide at Turn fourteen. Here's how it added up.

Verstappen passes Russell on the back straight. Then he brakes earlier than expected, and changes direction under braking.

Russell moves to the inside to avoid him, and makes contact.

The stewards call it unpredictable, below the standard expected. Their penalty: a driver warning. No grid drop. No points.

Separately, untelevised footage, shared on social media, appears to show Verstappen giving Russell the finger as he passed him. Widely reported. Not mentioned in the stewards' verdict.

Russell, after qualifying: pretty clear, I think. Somebody's got to stand up to that guy. Verstappen: nothing happened.

A warning, not a penalty. Right call? You be the steward. Follow for the next sum.
