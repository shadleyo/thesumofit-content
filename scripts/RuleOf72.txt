How fast does your money double? There's a five-second trick. It's called the Rule of seventy-two.

Divide seventy-two by your yearly return. At four percent, your money doubles about every eighteen years. At seven percent, about every ten. At ten percent, roughly every seven.

Now watch ten thousand dollars grow for forty years at each rate. At ten percent, it doubles in just over seven years. At four percent, it takes eighteen years to double just once.

After forty years, four percent turns ten thousand dollars into forty-eight thousand. Seven percent, about a hundred and fifty thousand. Ten percent, over four hundred and fifty thousand.

Triple the rate, and you end up with more than nine times the money.

Seventy-two, divided by your rate. Know your doubling time. That's the sum of it.
