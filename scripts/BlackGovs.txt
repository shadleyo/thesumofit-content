For the first time in American history, three elected Black governors will serve at the same time.

Before this week, Americans had elected only three Black governors, ever. Douglas Wilder, in Virginia, in 1989. Deval Patrick, in Massachusetts, in 2006. And Wes Moore, in Maryland, in 2022. None of them served together.

Now, the Associated Press has called three races. Wes Moore wins a second term in Maryland. Byron Donalds wins Florida, the first Black Republican governor in US history. And Keisha Lance Bottoms wins Georgia, the first Black woman ever elected governor.

Two parties. Three states. One first. That's the sum of it.
