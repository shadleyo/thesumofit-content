You get a raise. Three hundred dollars more a month. Most people spend it. What if you didn't?

Meet Ana and Ben. Both get the same raise. Ben upgrades his lifestyle. Ana invests the extra three hundred, and lives as before.

For the first few years, the difference is small. Around year nineteen, Ana's growth overtakes everything she put in.

After twenty-five years, Ben has spent ninety thousand dollars of raises. Ana has two hundred and forty-three thousand. Same raise. Different habit.

Next raise, try investing half. That's the sum of it.
