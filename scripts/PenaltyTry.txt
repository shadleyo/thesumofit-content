Should the Springboks have had a penalty try? Here's the law, and the numbers.

France led twenty-one, seven at half-time. Early in the second half, South Africa attack inside the French twenty-two.

Match reports describe a two-man overlap. Two attackers, and no defender in front of them.

The pass goes wide. A French hand knocks it down. Deliberately.

The referee gives a penalty, and a yellow card. But no penalty try.

Law eight says a penalty try is given when foul play prevents a probable try. Not a possible try. A probable one.

A penalty try is worth seven points. South Africa lost by two.

Two on none, inside the twenty-two. Probable, or not? You be the referee. Follow for the next sum.
