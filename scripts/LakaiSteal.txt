Twickenham. England lead twelve, nil. Then Peter Lakai steals the ball at the breakdown.

Then a knock-on at the base of the ruck. Two reports say it was Cam Roigard. A third says a pass from Fletcher Newell, and a stray boot.

That report adds: Nic Berry ruled New Zealand had already won the ball. So no penalty for the steal. England get a scrum, inside the All Blacks' twenty-two.

Ben Earl picks from the base and goes through. Converted. Nineteen, nil.

England finish with five tries to two. Thirty-seven, twelve. Their biggest winning margin over the All Blacks, ever.
