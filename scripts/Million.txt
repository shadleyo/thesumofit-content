How much do you need to invest each month to have a million dollars by sixty-five? It depends almost entirely on when you start.

Meet three savers. Ana starts at twenty-five. Ben starts at thirty-five. Cleo waits until forty-five.

Watch them race to a million. All three get there at sixty-five. But look how much harder the late starters have to climb.

To reach a million, Ana invests three hundred and eighty-one dollars a month. Ben needs eight hundred and twenty. Cleo needs one thousand nine hundred and twenty.

Ana puts in about a hundred and eighty-three thousand dollars. Cleo puts in four hundred and sixty-one thousand. Same million. The difference is time.

Every ten years you wait more than doubles the monthly bill. Start with whatever you can. That's the sum of it.
