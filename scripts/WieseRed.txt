A yellow, then a red, then a four-week ban. Here's how Jasper Wiese's card added up.

The referee shows yellow. Law nine twenty b: no contact above the line of the shoulders at a ruck. Several reports say it hit the head.

Then the bunker review upgrades it. A twenty-minute red. Wiese is off for good, but South Africa can bring on a replacement after twenty minutes.

The panel's sum: a mid-range offence, four weeks. Finish World Rugby's coaching programme, and it drops to three.

That's the Ireland Test, finals weekend, and two club games in Japan. He admitted it and accepted the ban.
